Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

This policy explains how KATZKATZ Clips (the "App") handles information. The App is a private tool operated by katzkatz17 (the "Owner") for the Owner's own KATZKATZ gaming YouTube channel and TikTok account. The App has one user, the Owner. It is not offered to the public and does not let anyone else sign in.

1. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App, the Owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information the App accesses and why

  • Google / YouTube account access (OAuth tokens). These let the App upload the Owner's clips to the Owner's channel, schedule or cancel them, check their processing status, read the status and live chat of the Owner's own live streams, and read analytics for the Owner's own videos.
  • The Owner's own YouTube data: metadata, status and performance statistics (for example views and watch time) of videos on the Owner's channel. These are used only to verify publishing and to choose better posting times.
  • Live chat of the Owner's own live streams. Read during the Owner's streams only to measure chat activity, which helps find highlight moments. Chat author identifiers are replaced with one-way hashes, and author names are not stored. Chat text is not sent to any AI service and is not published.
  • TikTok account access (OAuth tokens). These are used to read the Owner's basic profile (display name and avatar), read the posting options TikTok returns for the account, publish clips the Owner has approved, check post status, and read the list and statistics of the Owner's own TikTok videos.
  • Gmail and Google Drive (the Owner's own account). The App sends approval emails to the Owner and reads the Owner's replies to those emails, so it knows which clips to publish. It applies labels to processed messages, and it does not read other emails. Temporary preview videos are stored in a Drive folder that the App creates. Each is shared by an unlisted link inside the approval email and deleted automatically after at most 30 days.
  • Livestream recordings. These are the Owner's own recordings, stored locally on the Owner's computer.

3. Information about other people

The Owner's gameplay footage may show other players' in-game names. The App does not collect, store or process other people's personal information beyond the hashed chat identifiers described above, with one exception: the in-game usernames (gaming nicknames, not real names) of the Owner's squad teammates, as shown on the game's own screen, may be read from the footage to show the match's elimination counts in YouTube video thumbnails and in video titles. No other information about these players is collected. Clips are reviewed by the Owner before publishing, and spoken or on-screen personal details are bleeped or blurred.

4. Where information is stored

All information is stored locally on the Owner's computer, in files with access restricted to the App's own service account. Exceptions:

  • Content the Owner publishes to YouTube and TikTok.
  • Approval emails in the Owner's Gmail.
  • Temporary previews in the Owner's Drive.

Access tokens are never shared, published or sent anywhere other than the platform that issued them.

5. Sharing with third parties

Information is not sold, rented or shared for advertising. The only third-party processing is:

  • Anthropic (Claude API). Used to help prepare the Owner's clips and long-form videos. The text transcript of the Owner's own recordings (which may include the speech of people playing with the Owner) and short descriptions generated by the tool are sent to find highlight moments, check captions, and draft titles, descriptions and hashtags. This is processed under Anthropic's Commercial Terms and Privacy Policy. No data obtained through YouTube API Services or the TikTok API, no account tokens, chat messages or analytics data are sent.
  • Telegram (Bot API). A preview of each clip and its proposed texts are sent to the Owner's own private chat with the tool's bot, so the Owner can approve, edit or reject them. No other person receives these messages.
  • Google (YouTube, Gmail, Drive) and TikTok. Only to perform the actions described above, in the Owner's own accounts.

6. Retention

  • Data obtained through the YouTube API Services is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. After that it is kept only as aggregated, non-identifying statistics.
  • Preview files in Drive are deleted within 30 days.
  • Livestream recordings are kept on the Owner's computer only while they are still needed (for the month's recap video and the Owner's long-form videos, and for at least 14 days), and are then deleted automatically.
  • Clip files are deleted within 30 days after they are no longer needed.
  • Tokens are kept until the Owner revokes access.

7. Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google / YouTube / Gmail / Drive: revoke the App's access at Google Account → Third-party connections (security settings).
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, go to Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.
  • To request deletion of any stored data, contact the Owner at the address below. Data is deleted within 30 days.

8. Cookies and tracking

This website and the App use no cookies, analytics or tracking.

9. Children

The App is a private tool for its adult Owner and is not directed at children.

10. Changes

This policy is updated before any change in the data the App accesses or the services it uses. The date above shows the latest version.
