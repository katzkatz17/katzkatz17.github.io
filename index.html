KATZKATZ Clips

KATZKATZ Clips is a private tool used by katzkatz17, the owner of the KATZKATZ gaming gaming channel. It turns highlights from the owner's own Fortnite livestreams into short vertical clips, and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube channel and TikTok account. It is also used to publish the owner's own long-form videos to the same YouTube channel. Clips wait for the owner's approval before they are published; after the owner has approved enough clips, YouTube clips may be published automatically, and the owner can still cancel each one before it goes live. Every TikTok post is always confirmed by the owner.

The tool runs on the owner's home computer. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not access anyone else's account.

What it does

  • Finds highlight moments in the owner's own livestream recordings.
  • Edits them into vertical clips with Hebrew captions.
  • Sends the owner a private approval message for each clip (a private Telegram chat with the tool's bot, with email as a backup).
  • Publishes approved clips to the owner's YouTube channel (YouTube Data API) and TikTok account (TikTok Content Posting API). On TikTok, the owner confirms each post and chooses its privacy level.
  • Reads performance statistics of the owner's own videos to choose better posting times.

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
